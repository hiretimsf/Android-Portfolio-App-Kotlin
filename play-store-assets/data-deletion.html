HireTimSF Data Deletion

This page explains how users of the HireTimSF Android app can request deletion of app-related data.

How to request deletion

Send an email to hiretimsf@gmail.com with the subject line HireTimSF Data Deletion Request.

Include the app name, the approximate date you used the app, and any details that help identify the data you want deleted.

Data that can be deleted

The HireTimSF app does not provide account creation, login, comments, uploads, messaging, purchases, or user-generated content. Deletion requests apply to app-related analytics, diagnostics, crash, and performance data that can be associated with the app usage and that is available for deletion through the services used by the app.

Data that may be kept

Some aggregated, anonymized, security, abuse-prevention, or legally required records may be retained when they cannot reasonably be associated with an individual user or when retention is required for legitimate purposes.

Response time

Requests are reviewed and handled as soon as reasonably possible. If additional information is needed to process the request, a reply will be sent to the email address used for the request.
